## Carried over from 2025

Five teams renamed, so these are written with both names. Use the current one;
reach for the old one only when the joke needs it.

- **2011 Ryan Braun (jxllokreflo) went 0-14 last season as the 2024 White
  Sox.** Fourteen games, fourteen losses. The defining fact of the league's
  recent history, and a new team name does not wash it off.
- **Los Penguinos (zazach) posted 52.8 in week 14 as Chicken Salad**, the

  starters on 0.0.

- **Justin Jefferson Davis (rthd20) scored 126.61 in week 14 and lost by 36**, as Henry
