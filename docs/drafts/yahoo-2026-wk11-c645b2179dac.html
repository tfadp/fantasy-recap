The Race for First

Six teams at 7–4, and points-for is the only thing keeping them in line: Schmul Gone Wild 1079.8, ‘Ster the Pot 1077.05, Ass in a Box 1028.55, Dread the Bred 1017.5, Gowanus Heights Hex 1017.3, Busta Skreen 992.7. First and second are split by 2.75. Fourth and fifth are split by 0.2, less than Michael Mayer scored this week, and Michael Mayer scored 0.4.

First place in this league is a hot potato. Six different teams have held it in eleven weeks: Anchor Down, ‘Ster the Pot, Canes to the Cup, THE FURY, Ass in a Box and now Schmul Gone Wild. ‘Ster the Pot has been No. 1 three separate times - after weeks 2, 4 and 9 - and has never kept it for two weeks running. Every time the crown goes on, it slides right back off.

Ass in a Box owned first for four of the five weeks before this one, then lost by 22.0 to a 5–6 Krispy and dropped to third. Schmul Gone Wild was ninth after week 7 and has won four straight since. Dread the Bred started 1–3, has gone 6–1, and is 7–4 despite being outscored on the season, 1017.5 to 1029.65. Busta Skreen has scored the fewest of the six at 992.7 and allowed a league-low 890.65 - the schedule has been doing its homework for it.

The former kings are a cautionary tale. Anchor Down led after week 1 and is 4–7. Canes to the Cup was 3–0 and in first after week 3, and has gone 1–7 since, including five straight losses. THE FURY led after week 5 and has gone 1–5 since, to 5–6.

And sitting seventh, two games out of a six-way tie, is Philly Special: a league-high 1119.35 points for, 1134.1 points against, 5–6. Best offense in the league, watching the party through the window.
