Week 10 Recap - Bring It On

Tuesday, November 24

Won it with the lights off. Jaxon Smith-Njigba (23.1) and a Bills defense (12) were plenty, because 67.3 was never going to be enough. C.J. Stroud (22.55) spent the afternoon on the bench behind Drake Maye (3.5) and it cost Busta Skreen absolutely nothing; both now 6–4.

Dread the Bred (109.7) def. Sleeping With The Fishes (89.65)

Mahomes and a placekicker. Patrick Mahomes (38.9) and Cam Little (21) finished one-two, which tells you how the rest of the lineup spent its Sunday. Parker Washington (21.2) led Sleeping With The Fishes into another 20.05-point hole; Dread the Bred is 6–4 on two straight wins, Sleeping With The Fishes is 4–6 on two straight losses.

Schmul Gone Wild (137.3) def. Krispy (83)

Biggest beating of the week. Jahmyr Gibbs (37.2) and Emmett Johnson (29.1) built a new high of 137.3, the best score in the league since Philly Special's 140.7 in week 8. Trevor Lawrence threw for 30.3 and Krispy ran a 100% efficient lineup into a 54.3-point loss, which leaves nothing in the app to blame; Schmul Gone Wild is 6–4 on three straight wins, Krispy is 4–6.
